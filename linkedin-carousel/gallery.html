    House rules

    1. 4:5 portrait, 1080 × 1350. It takes the most room in the mobile feed.
    2. 6–10 slides, one idea per slide, roughly 20–40 words each.
    3. Body text 45px or larger. If you can't read it on a phone, it fails.
    4. Slide 2 changes the pace. The drop from slide 1 to 2 is the biggest.
    5. Two typefaces and one accent colour per carousel. Same closing card every time.
    6. No stock gradients, no decorative AI images, no emoji bullets.
    7. Judge styles against your own average saves and reposts, not big creators' numbers.

    Cited on the slides

    Make your own

    The engine lives in linkedin-carousel/. Edit the copy in content/growth-examples.js, set your name at the top, then run:

    node export.mjs --check

    Each carousel comes out as out/<id>.pdf to upload as a LinkedIn document post, plus a PNG per slide.
